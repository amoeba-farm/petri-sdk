Public operations are `writer_liquidity_initialize`, `writer_liquidity_add`, `writer_liquidity_remove`, `writer_liquidity_sweep`. All take `{owner,sleeve,seriesIndex}`. Add also takes `issueAmountAtoms` and entries `{binId,maximumOptionAmountAtoms,maximumQuoteAmountAtoms}`. Remove takes entries `{binId,optionAmountAtoms,quoteAmountAtoms}`. Each action has 1..8 strictly ascending bins in 1..2048 and a positive amount of at least one asset per entry. Issuance may be zero; positive issuance is whole 1,000,000-atom contracts and equals the option-placement maximum total. Remove has no separate minima, share amount, nonce or recipient. Removed options retire through native issuer custody and quote returns to sleeve cash.

Client methods are `writerOperations.prepareLiquidityInitialize/Add/Remove/Sweep`, at `/dlmm/writer-sleeves/liquidity/{initialize,add,remove,sweep}/prepare`. Operator methods `prepareLiquidityPolicyBegin/Append/Seal` use `/dlmm/writer-sleeves/liquidity/policy/{begin,append,seal}/prepare`. Begin binds explicit management authority, expected hash, monthly/transaction caps, reserve-release spend ratio and price-separation ticks. Append binds a contiguous prefix of conservative claim values, seller floors and per-series caps. No numeric policy default is supplied by the SDK.

`writerOperations.liquidity({owner,sleeve,seriesIndex})` reads `/dlmm/writer-sleeves/:sleeve/liquidity?owner=...&seriesIndex=...`. Its closed typed DTO is the frozen contract in Lean `docs/WRITER_LIQUIDITY_API_20260909.md`: policy lifecycle, canonical bindings, frozen manager/actor authority, sleeve and selected-series accounting, LP quote rights, budgets, raw bins and finalized slot/digest. Historical net primary premium is distinct from current cash and LP quote custody. Refund/claim/close/depositor controls retain their own semantics.
